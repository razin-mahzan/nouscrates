# Nouscrates

**A voluntary oath of honor for the humane use and sharing of AI knowledge.**

Nouscrates is a personal ethical pledge concerning the use and transmission of knowledge about artificial intelligence and automation.

Its central principle is narrow: a competent person should not be deprived of their livelihood solely because AI can now perform their work better. Where technological change makes existing work unnecessary, those with the ability to influence the transition should make a genuine effort to provide alternatives such as retraining, redeployment, time to develop a new role, or other meaningful transition support.

Nouscrates does **not** oppose AI, automation, technological progress, or the use of AI instead of hiring for work that has not yet been assigned to a person. It is concerned specifically with how existing people are treated when technology makes their current duties unnecessary.

A second principle concerns knowledge. A person who takes the oath voluntarily limits their own active teaching, promotion, elaboration, or transmission of AI knowledge that could facilitate such displacement, except to people who accept the same responsibility.

The oath applies only to actions within a person's practical and moral control. It does not require violation of law, employment obligations, institutional duties, or attempts to suppress information that the person does not control.

The oath is a **word of honor**, not a legal instrument. It creates no legal rights or obligations.
