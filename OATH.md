# The Nouscrates Oath

I give my word of honor that I will not use my knowledge of artificial intelligence to advocate for, facilitate, or cause the dismissal of an otherwise competent person solely because artificial intelligence can perform that person's work better.

Where artificial intelligence makes a person's existing duties unnecessary, and where I have meaningful influence over the outcome, I will make a genuine effort to preserve that person's livelihood through reasonable alternatives such as retraining, redeployment, time to develop a new role, or other meaningful transition support.

This oath does not require the creation of work that would not otherwise exist, nor does it prohibit using artificial intelligence instead of hiring a person for work that has not already been entrusted to one.

I will not knowingly teach, promote, elaborate upon, or actively transmit AI knowledge that could materially facilitate such displacement to another person unless that person freely accepts this same oath, in this same canonical form, and undertakes the same responsibility when sharing such knowledge with others.

This obligation applies only to actions and knowledge within my practical and moral control. It does not require me to violate law, employment obligations, institutional duties, or other legitimate obligations, nor does it require me to prevent dissemination of information that I do not control.

This is a voluntary word of honor. It creates no legal rights or obligations.

I take this oath freely.
